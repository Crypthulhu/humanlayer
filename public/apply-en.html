01The role

What it means to be a HumanLayer Sentinel

Being a HumanLayer Sentinel is not consulting. It’s not executing tasks. It’s not “helping an AI”. It’s committing your authority, where it matters.

  1. 01Intervening only when a decision carries human accountability.
  2. 02Signing, approving or rejecting based on AI-prepared context.
  3. 03Leaving an auditable trace of your professional judgment.
  4. 04Being paid for the decision — not for time spent.

02Earnings

How much can you earn?

Up to 60% of the price of every decision goes to you. Compensation is indexed to the level of responsibility involved, the rarity of your qualification and the regulatory framework of the decision.

03Protection

Accountability, framework and protection.

You never decide blindly. You never decide outside your scope. You never decide without traceability. Each decision:

is strictly limited to your authorization scope;

relies on documented context;

leaves a timestamped, auditable trace;

is embedded in a clear contractual framework.

HumanLayer does not automate your accountability. It frames and protects it.

How are applications reviewed?

HumanLayer does not hire in bulk. We qualify profiles capable of assuming human accountability.

Applications are automatically evaluated against strict criteria: experience, authorization, jurisdiction, area of expertise.

Profiles matching active or high-value needs are contacted for a focused interview. Other applications may be kept in a qualified pool and activated later, without any time commitment.
